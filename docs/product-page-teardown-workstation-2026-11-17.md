# Product page teardown for Workstation (2026-11-17)

Nate's references for the Workstation page (the first page written for one buyer):

- Rivian [R1T](https://rivian.com/r1t). Nate named it the closest to Workstation and Cargo, and it was already studied in `product-page-teardown-2026-11-16.md`.
- Rivian [R2](https://rivian.com/r2) and [R1S](https://rivian.com/r1s).
- Tesla [Cybertruck](https://www.tesla.com/cybertruck), also named the closest to Workstation and Cargo.
- Ring [Jobsite Security](https://ring.com/jobsite-security). Nate loves it: "simple. Lots of whitespace. Very clean."

All were read in Chrome on 2026-11-17.

## Ring Jobsite Security (the look Nate wants)

The page is about 5,600 px tall, a third of the Rivian and Tesla pages.

| # | Section | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | Job site photo at dusk. A small category label, a plain promise ("Your Jobsite, protected."), one sentence and one button, all on the left |
| 2 | Benefits | Four centered columns on a pale band, each a thin line icon, a bold title and two lines of gray text (peace of mind, deterrence, affordability, efficiency) |
| 3 | Any kind of site | One centered headline, then a row of photo cards by site type (construction, parking lots, shopping centers) |
| 4 | Meet the products | Each product on white on one side, with the name, starting price, a short paragraph, two buttons and a list of features with line icons. Each feature row opens to one line. The sides alternate. |
| 5 | Footnotes | Small gray text |

What makes it work is that every section does one job, the headlines are plain, there's a lot of white, there are no dark sections, and the icons are thin.

## Tesla Cybertruck

The page is about 18,500 px.

- **Hero.** The custom wordmark, two buttons (Order Now, Compare Models) and three stats along the bottom of the photo.
- **Section bar.** Once the hero scrolls away, a slim bar shows the name on the left and three actions on the right.
- **Body.** Mostly white. Each chapter is a sentence-case headline, one sentence of text and a large inset photo or video.
  - The panels have one cut corner that echoes the truck's shape.
  - Video carousels carry a short caption under each card ("Bring It All", "Power On Tap").
- **Stat rows.** They follow the big panels: a large number, a small unit, a gray label and thin dividers. Examples are 2.6 s, 11,000 lbs and 845 hp.
- **Ending.** The page turns dark for Powershare (power out to tools and the home, with its own stat row), then the full spec grid on dark.

## Rivian R2

The page is about 33,700 px.

- **Hero.** A giant "R" and "2" frame a video window cut in the R2's headlight shape, on light gray.
- **Floating section bar.** The same one as on R1T.
- **Opening.** Three captioned photo cards ("R2 is designed for the adventurous").
- **Body.** Full-bleed video breaks, and carousels with a numbered caption and arrows.
- **Size it up.** Shown on dark with a two-column dimension table.
- **Close.** "Your R2 is waiting" with Reserve and Design yours, then the FAQ.

## Rivian R1S

The page is about 20,000 px. It has the same structure as R1T, but opens with a carousel of tall photo cards with the feature name set large on each ("Captain's chairs", "Charging and range").

## What the SPOG Workstation draft took

The draft is at `/test-workstation`.

- **From Ring.**
  - The top half of the page is light, with the space and calm Nate asked for. The numbers and the close are dark, like the end of the Cybertruck page.
  - Four outcome columns sit right under the hero, written as outcomes for a GC: open on day one, power before temp power, no generator, and signal past the grid.
  - The product block shows the picture on one side and a list of features on the other, with each row opening to one line.
- **From Cybertruck.**
  - The three numbers a GC asks about first sit along the bottom of the hero.
  - Stat rows use thin dividers.
  - The numbers (power and size) come together in one dark chapter near the end.
- **From the Rivian pages.** The floating section bar, and "Size it up" as a to-scale drawing on dark.
- **SPOG's own.**
  - "One trailer. Three problems solved." is set as a sum (site office + generator + security = Workstation), because that's the GC pitch in one line.
  - The heading is the approved brand line.

## What SPOG changed or left out

- **No product-on-white render.** The Workstation studio render shows a door decal the naming rules ban, so the to-scale line drawing does that job until there's a clean render.
- **No site-type photo cards.** There are no real photos by job type yet. Add them when Robert's shoot covers ground-up, rural and renovation sites.
- **No prices and no configurator link,** same as the other product pages.
- **No cut-corner panels.** They belong to the Cybertruck's shape. The work look uses a gold bar before each section label and heavy uppercase headings instead.
- **The remote office buyer gets one quiet band near the end.** It links to its own landing page once that exists, so the page stays a GC page.
