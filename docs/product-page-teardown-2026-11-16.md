Source: rivian.com/r1t, read in Chrome at 1512 px wide. Page height about 17,500 px.

## What SPOG took

- The hero pattern: product name huge, one line, two actions. No stats in the hero.
- The floating section bar that replaces the masthead after the hero.
- "Get to know" as one sentence followed by features.
- Photo breaks between information sections.
- "Size it up" as a line drawing on dark with the spec table under it.
- "Find the one for you" across the lineup at the bottom.

## What SPOG changed

- **Power replaces drive modes.** The shared SPOG message is the power system, so the
  interactive section shows how long each device runs on each battery size. Numbers come from
  SPOG's own runtime guide. They're shown as stat tiles, not bars, because they range from
  under 2 hours to hundreds.
- **Size it up shows the trailer behind a pickup.** Buyers think about towing and parking, so
  the trailer is drawn to scale behind a full-size pickup and grows as you pick a length.
  Only the length is labeled.
- **No prices.** Rivian leads with lease and starting prices. SPOG pricing is private.
- **No configurator link.** `/configure/cargo` uses made-up sample prices. Link it from the
  product pages only after real pricing is approved.
- **Feature tiles instead of a photo carousel.** There aren't enough real product photos yet,
  and photos of one trailer can't stand in for another. Light tiles with line icons carry the
  features until Robert's shoot.
- **Close and FAQ reuse the homepage sections,** so the walkthrough form and Reserve work the same everywhere.
